**AGILE SAPIENS** is a scientific popular monograph examining how classical literature intuitively described principles that modern management later commercialized as revolutionary methodologies.

We trace the literary roots of business practices — from Jules Verne's expeditions to Borges' parables, from Shelley's Frankenstein to Wells' Time Machine — through rigorous textual analysis with corporate irony as the accompanying voice. The thesis: most of what we sell as "innovation" was first written down by novelists, often centuries before McKinsey caught up.

## Method

**Voice:** Literary Business Analysis — systematic workplace insights through classical texts.
**Structure:** 11 chapters (Chapter 6 in two parts) + 3 intermezzi, organised in three acts (Origins → Present → Future).
**Apparatus:** preface, afterword, methodology note, full bibliography by chapter, glossary of substituted terms, subject index, colophon.

## Principles

- **Evidence-based:** every claim is backed by primary or peer-reviewed sources.
- **Independent analysis:** no vendor bias or corporate agenda.
- **Accessible language:** complex ideas explained without jargon.
